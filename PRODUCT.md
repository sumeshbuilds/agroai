# AgroAI

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Farmers in Kalahandi, Odisha, using Odia or English, including outdoors and on weak mobile connections.

## Product Purpose

One application with Land Security (farm mapping, field cameras, animal/person alerts) and Plant Security (crop selection, lifecycle guidance, weather advice, and plant assessment).

## Operating Context

The pilot uses spare phone cameras. Browser capture requires HTTPS, power, connectivity, and an active camera page. Existing farmers, fields, crops, camera sessions, and reports must survive the upgrade.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The application already has saved farm boundaries, on-device person/animal detection, snapshot monitoring, dashboard alarms, Open-Meteo forecasts, crop-stage guides, and weekly reports. Satellite scene analysis, dependable background push, reviewed pesticide catalogs, and photo diagnosis are separate capabilities, not implied by those foundations. The budget is free-first; paid infrastructure requires approval. Satellite imagery is periodic and cloud-dependent. Model-derived forecasts are not field-sensor measurements. Camera detections do not establish identity or intent.

## Brand Commitments

The user approved a complete gamified redesign inspired by the supplied Brainly references: soft rounded cards, lime and purple, friendly character artwork, bold readable typography, and playful completion feedback. Use original AgroAI branding, not Brainly's mascot or marks.

## Evidence on Hand

`v2.md` contains the agreed roadmap. Existing live features are represented by the repository's camera, weather, map, crop, and authentication modules. There are no verified crop-health benchmarks, satellite observations, or approved pesticide-product matches to fabricate.

## Product Principles

- Preserve farmer data and working features.
- Make Land Security and Plant Security immediately findable.
- Reward care routines, not danger or pesticide consumption.
- Distinguish live, unavailable, estimated, and illustrative information.
- Keep urgent actions readable and serious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Odia and English, large outdoor-readable controls, keyboard access, reduced motion, and explicit offline/error states. Agricultural and treatment translations require local review before a production launch.
