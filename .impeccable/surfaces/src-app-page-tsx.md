# AgroAI landing page

Mode: Persuade. Scope: public marketing page at `/`, with the signed-in workstation moving to `/dashboard`.

## Audience and job

Farmers in Kalahandi, Odisha, often on a weak phone connection and outdoors in bright light. They need to understand the two promises — Land Security and Plant Security — and trust that the app works with the phone and signal they already have. Success is creating a farm account or signing in, with no exaggerated capability claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Prove the workstation in the first viewport with one composed field-console scene; refuse the generic hero plus equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm porcelain paper, deep forest ink, lime field panels, plum progress, crisp survey geometry and product UI as the imagery. Bricolage Grotesque display over DM Sans; 20–28px panels, hairline rules, tabular field data, no decorative glass.

STORY: See the land and crop being watched and guided, understand the two securities, then create an account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Paper-ground nav, then an oversized two-line display headline, short proof paragraph and lime primary action on the left. On the right, a dark field console holding a satellite field polygon, camera pin, confirmed animal detection, alert toast and weather chip. No kicker. One authored motion: the alert arrives and the camera sweep settles.

FORM: Extension of the established Field Ops world, first of three extension candidates; code-led, no concept roll because the visual world is settled.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
